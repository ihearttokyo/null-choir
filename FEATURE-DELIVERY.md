## Independent adversarial quality gate (26 October 2026)

- Define the feature, its actual user requirements, intended-use evidence and weighted rubric before review. Preserve the full goal; do not redefine a feature to hide failed requirements.
- An independent adversarial testing/review agent, not the feature's implementer, owns the rating. Feature quality target: **9.2/10** under its declared rubric, without rounding a lower score up or inventing new per-component thresholds.
- The orchestrator receives ratings/evidence. Implementers receive only actionable defects, reproduction evidence and PASS/FAIL—not numerical results or rating files. Rating artifacts stay outside implementation/publication payloads; authors must not read them.
- Once PASS is reached, freeze the accepted feature's source/artifact identity and STOP hillclimbing it. Reopen only for a demonstrated material regression caused by later work or an actual changed requirement—not cosmetic opportunities or more score-seeking tests.
- Run proportionate affected checks. Preserve real repository protections and required checks; never fabricate green results or weaken security/data-integrity boundaries.
- Known security flaws, data loss, installation failure, or broken primary paths remain release blockers. Non-blocking bugs go into a concrete backlog with severity and owner; they do NOT indefinitely hold deployment or trigger another full regression matrix.
- Deploy coherent passing upgrades as honestly labelled previews when other goal features remain unfinished. A preview is not completion of the full Alpha milestone. Batch coherent changes/PRs rather than serial tiny releases.
