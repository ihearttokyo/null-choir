# NC08 purpose-marker integration

Eight retained Blender models now distinguish ammunition, health, armor, seals,
weapons, records, checkpoints and exits. The level uses the existing scene cache
and removal boundary. Authored IDs, cells, collection thresholds, collision,
inventory and save rules are unchanged. Weapon recovery names the exact weapon.

The adopted generation is `marker-production-01`, whose family manifest SHA-256
is `5591903c21e9f6a8be435f137942595fe55a6a8bf6a711511be46d709a44b213`.
Its eight editable sources and eight exports retain their original pins.
Historical helper bytes are data-only archives under `source/art/recipe_history`;
current authoring still rejects helper drift. No old output was repinned.
The 16 required previews and geometry receipts are explicitly delivered under
their existing `builds/marker-art` paths, despite the general build-directory ignore.

## Verified support

- Native production factory: old generic-scene assertion fails, then all eight
  maps pass purpose identity, authored anchors/scale and visual-only removal.
- Same-art meshes/materials share ownership; transforms and removal remain
  independent. All eight levels release their marker resources on teardown.
- Existing full/over-cap ammunition, one-shot grants, interaction occlusion,
  story collection and recovery rules remain covered by native fixtures.
- The interaction fixture now drains its queued level before exit; its previous
  PASS text accompanied an actual resource-leak failure and was not accepted.
- Settings, Controls and difficulty retain their selected menu at both phases
  of the placement wait. This does not prove foreground mouse capture.
- File validation covers 111 assets and 113 exports. The original 103 manifest
  entries are unchanged. Geometry and portability suites pass 13 and 16 cases.
- Independent correctness and Ponytail reviews cover runtime, fixtures,
  provenance and portable evidence delivery.

## Remaining NC08 acceptance

Blood/ivory geometry, ammunition-family and weapon emblems, and shared HUD/survey
glyphs remain separate unfinished work. Recognition at actual game size and
4/8/16 metres, dark/bright backgrounds, grayscale and color-deficiency diagnostics
is not certified here. Current GPU, standalone-app and human recognition checks
remain unrun. This integration is not full campaign or final visual acceptance.

Rollback restores the previous level marker factory and manifest; retain the
original scenery capsule assets and all source references.
