## Scope and rollback

This change verifies continuous earned entry, observer retirement and bounded idle
continuation. Its direct callback probes are synthetic, not earned combat, heard
audio or rendered pixels. Fourth combat/routes, the campaign matrix, physical
controls/capture, visual and audio quality, performance and the current standalone
app remain separate requirements. The pin-refusal fixture requires an intentionally
mismatched pin; it is not a default valid-pin test.

Rollback restores the three prior test files and removes the new fixtures/document.
The accepted campaign source and original assets remain unchanged. Raw mutant
sources, receipts and isolated native profiles stay outside Git.
