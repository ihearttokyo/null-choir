## Delivered since Preview 06.1

**A cathedral with distinct threats.** Eight original directional enemy/boss bodies and role-specific attack telegraphs are now consolidated into the release. Earn Blood and Ivory seals from their bearers and face an exit warden in each chapter. **Earn the arsenal.** The six weapons have differentiated shot effects, from close impact and Rivet fire to scatter, repeater, Marrow and Choir attacks. Equipment belongs to authored carriers rather than loose floor guns: defeat the carrier, then physically walk over its finite weapon/ammunition/armor drop. At capacity, the unused remainder stays pending rather than disappearing. Save→title→Continue preserves the pending equipment and exact remaining amount. Seals also collect by walking over the earned drop; E remains door/context interaction.

**32 service points, not 32 free resets.** Eight textured 3D healing-machine families serve four floor-bolted points per chapter. Face a usable nearby machine and press **Space** to transfer from its finite stock; a live gauge shows what remains. Stock and pending drops are saved. Healing does not bypass ownership, range, line of sight or physical support checks, and holding the action is not an unlimited refill.

**Scoped precision, not universal anatomy.** The Worker's authored pressure-clutch hit can cause its finite vent response. The Nursery Warden offers a brief, masked front pressure-node opportunity for precision damage. These are not a general headshot or anatomical-weak-point system, nor blanket bonuses for every body, rear fire or all visible pixels.

**Momentum in the dark.** Doom-inspired movement supports a more forceful descent. Local apparatus and actor/equipment-owned practical light helps machines, critical carriers and earned drops remain readable, while dark negative space and the moody cathedral lighting remain intentional. No Doom maps, sprites or songs are copied.

The earlier forward-aiming Rivet Pistol, original Pixel Music Garden, eight living thresholds, chapter paintings, raised routes/Ossuary crossing and eight offline Suno selections remain included. ## Controls

WASD moves; mouse looks; left click fires. **E** operates doors and contextual interactables. **Walk over enemy equipment and earned seals** to collect them. **Space** uses a usable nearby finite healing machine. Q/Tab cycles acquired weapons; 1–6 selects them; Escape pauses. Default gamepad X uses healing machinery; controls remain rebindable. Saving failure keeps the current session and offers an explicit discard choice instead of silently losing progress.
