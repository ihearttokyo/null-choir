# Narrative and recovered records

## Story contract

The cathedral was built around a living speaker who asked to be held while workers escaped. The original restraint worked. Later service revisions restore movement while reporting renewed containment. The player, recovery technician Ellis Marr, is the ambulatory continuation of that held patient. The command channel originates inside the organism. Its change of intention, the patient's earlier life and the organism's origin remain unresolved.

Boundary Works is a fallible human construction and maintenance institution. Recovery Office is its compromised administrative authority. The seven architectural traditions retain separate origins; the hostile organism appropriates their structures and service practices. Fictional appropriation drives the antagonism; real religious communities and a universal secret doctrine do not.

The silent player supplies acknowledgements through work and contact. Blood and ivory form an unordered authorization: both are required, either may be recovered first. The Nursery warden and Choir avatar remain local manifestations. Defeating the avatar removes the final obstruction without killing the entire buried organism. Save/checkpoint recovery does not establish a resurrection mechanic or time loop.
