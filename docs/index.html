Keep moving. Look closer.

Six weapons give you reasons to close the distance, hold a corridor, or save a few rounds for whatever is waiting below. Watch your ammunition, recover both seals, and earn the next descent.

Between fights, search for the records left behind. Each level has an optional discovery. You can stop and read; you can also keep running. The mystery waits for you.

Eight levels.
Less familiar ground.

Pixel-art characters inhabit three-dimensional worlds built in Blender. The route runs from industrial intake to the Heart, with a different offline score for every level.

Alpha checkpoint, not a finished release

Expect bugs, hitches and balance changes. Full campaign and 120 FPS performance are not yet verified. Fullscreen performance is an active problem under investigation. Physical gamepad coverage and final soundtrack listening are still being tested.

Pause and settings

Settings includes sensitivity, audio levels and rebinding. Choose Sound test from the title menu to explore the music and fractal animation.

Your HUD tracks health, armor, ammunition, six weapon slots, Blood and Ivory seals, and recovered story records. Dim weapon icons are locked; bright seals are recovered.
