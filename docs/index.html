Before you play

Alpha checkpoint, not a finished release

Download the Mac ZIP above, extract it, and open NULL CHOIR. This download is Developer ID-signed and Apple-notarized, with its verification ticket included. If you downloaded the earlier blocked build, download this version and open the newly extracted app. Keep your system security protections enabled.

Expect bugs, hitches and balance changes. Full campaign and 120 FPS performance are not yet verified. Physical gamepad coverage and musical quality are still being tested. Save progress through the game's pause menu.

Pause and settings

Settings includes sensitivity, audio levels and rebinding.

Your HUD tracks health, armor, ammunition, six weapon slots, Blood and Ivory seals, and recovered story records. Dim weapon icons are locked; bright seals are recovered.
